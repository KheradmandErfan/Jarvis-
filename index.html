Jarvis Assistant – Free AI Voice Assistant Web App

Jarvis Assistant is a free, browser-based AI voice assistant inspired by Iron Man's JARVIS. Talk to it using your microphone or type your commands: ask it questions and get AI-powered answers, translate words between languages, do quick math calculations, check the time and date anywhere in the world, play rock-paper-scissors, open apps and websites, and search the web — all through an animated sci-fi HUD interface. No installation required, works directly in your browser.
